# Partner pilot briefs

Prepared 2026-10-16. These are proposals, not existing integrations or commitments.

## Cognitum — Monday October 19

Hypothesis: Soapbox provides admitted execution pools for selected Cognitum jobs or
agent environments; their runtime and enterprise distribution supply demand.
Persistent workspaces require Phase 4; do not promise them from the current batch pilot.

Propose one approved, bounded batch workload first. Agree on:
- Runtime/model/image and licensing; CPU/GPU/RAM needs and input/output sizes.
- Which inputs may leave customer hardware; exact permitted pools and locations.
- Expected volume, latency, acceptance criteria and measurable success.
- Which system owns scheduling, policy, customer support and billing.
- A demonstration using explicit API integration, followed by a paid pilot if useful.

Cognitum also has orchestration/memory/agent-desktop functionality. Keep interfaces narrow
and preserve independent product ownership. Advisory credentials do not establish workload demand.

## Liquid Compute — Wednesday October 21

Hypothesis: qualification and buyer access for a compatible subset of node supply.
First determine whether buyers need datacenter reservations or interruptible consumer tasks.

Ask for one buyer specification: GPU models/VRAM, interconnect, location, minimum uptime,
reservation length, qualification procedure, price basis, failure/refund rules and settlement flow.
Ask whether Liquid is the buyer, broker/distributor or qualification provider for that pilot.

Their published GCAT currently excludes consumer GeForce RTX GPUs. That establishes a
qualification gap; it does not prove they reject every possible consumer-capacity product.
Do not present all Bitterbot peers as qualified compute supply.

Primary background:
- https://liquidcompute.com/gpu-test
- https://liquidcompute.com/product-registry

## Shared success criterion

Leave each meeting with a workload owner, acceptance contract, resource requirements,
commercial sponsor and next technical test. Measure eligible opted-in capacity before
quoting supply. No external messages, meetings, accounts, credentials or deployments
were created by this implementation.
